❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a cab?

Open Book Cab, enter your trip details and confirm.

Is OTP required?

No. Our planned customer login uses a PIN.

What is Ride PIN?

A unique 4-digit PIN generated for a ride. Share it with the driver only when you are ready to start.

Can I share my trip?

Yes. The Safety Center will provide the Share Trip feature.

Is there an SOS feature?

Yes. The Safety Center contains an emergency SOS option.
