Terms & Conditions

Please read before booking.

1. Booking

Booking requests are subject to vehicle and driver availability. Confirmation may require contact from our team.

2. Fare

Displayed fares are estimates unless specifically confirmed. Toll, parking and applicable charges may be extra.

3. Service

Passengers should follow reasonable safety instructions and verify vehicle details before starting the trip.

After confirmation

No-show

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Darshana Sethu Cab.

How can I book a cab?

Enter pickup and destination, get a fare estimate and continue with the booking details.

Do I need to login?

No. You can book without a customer login. Your booking ID and self-created Ride PIN identify your booking.

What is the Ride PIN?

It is your private 4-digit code used to confirm your ride. Do not share it before the driver arrives.

How do Sethu Rewards work?

Eligible completed rides can earn Sethu Points. Redemption rules may apply.
