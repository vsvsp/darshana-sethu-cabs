❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a cab?

Open Book Cab, enter your trip details and confirm.

What is Ride PIN?

A unique 4-digit PIN generated for the ride. Share it with the Driver Partner only when you are ready to start.

Can I share my trip?

Yes. The Safety Center will provide Share Trip.

Is there an SOS feature?

Yes. The Safety Center contains an emergency SOS option.

How is my fare handled?

The platform model is commission-based. Darshana Sethu Cab receives its applicable commission rather than the full ride amount.
